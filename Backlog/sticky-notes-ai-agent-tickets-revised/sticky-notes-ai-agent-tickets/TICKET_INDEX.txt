Execution ownership and safe parallelization
- Execute SN-06 through SN-10 sequentially in the same working context.
- Do not assign movement, resizing, creation, and trash deletion to separate agents, branches, or independent patches.
- The interaction engine must have one owner until all four core interactions work because these tickets share useBoardGestures.ts, its public API, preview state, and terminal commit/cancel behavior.
- Safe parallel work is limited to SN-03 geometry, SN-04 reducer, and SN-12 storage after their stated dependencies are satisfied.
- SN-10 depends on movement because deletion is a movement commit branch.
- Integration, the final focused test pass, and README should be finalized only after shared behavior stabilizes.

If a conflict appears, preserve must-ship behavior and the frozen architecture. Do not expand scope.
